The studio

A former carpentry workshop in Montreuil, one wheel, two kilns and shelves full of drying pieces.

Inès, from graphic designer to ceramicist

I spent eight years in front of a screen before taking a throwing class "just to try". Two years later, I left the agency to train as a ceramicist.

Today I make everyday pieces: objects you use daily and that age well. I also work with restaurants and shops that want tableware that reflects who they are.

The five lives of a piece

About six weeks pass between a lump of clay and a finished piece.

  1. Throwing

    The clay is centred on the wheel, then pulled up in a few minutes.

    Day 1

  2. Drying

    Slow and away from draughts, to avoid cracks.

    1 to 2 weeks

  3. Bisque firing

    A first firing at 980 °C makes the piece strong but porous.

    24 hours

  4. Glazing

    Dipped, brushed or poured: glaze brings colour and makes the piece watertight.

    1 day

  5. Glaze firing

    At 1,280 °C the glaze melts and the piece becomes durable for years.

    36 hours

Frequently asked questions

Are your pieces dishwasher safe?

Yes, all tableware is high-fired stoneware and is dishwasher and microwave safe. Lamps and decorative pieces should be cleaned by hand.

How long does a custom order take?

Allow four to eight weeks depending on quantity and the firing schedule. For a wedding or a restaurant opening, let me know as early as possible.

Do you ship?

I ship across France and Europe, packed in recycled cardboard and crumpled paper. Collection from the studio is free.

Do you work with restaurants?

Yes, it's a large part of my work. We meet first to develop prototypes together before production.

Can I give a class as a gift?

Yes, every class is available as a gift voucher valid for one year. Just mention it in the contact form.
