Inès Garnier, ceramicist in Montreuil, Paris

Clay, hands, and a lot of patience

Tableware, vases and lamps, wheel-thrown by hand in local stoneware. Every piece is unique, and most can be made to order for you.

See the work Order a piece

What guides the studio

  • Clay from nearby

    Stoneware dug in Burgundy, 250 km from the studio. Every scrap is rehydrated and reused.

  • Nothing is mass-made

    Every piece is thrown, trimmed and glazed by hand. No two bowls are ever quite twins.

  • Mindful firings

    An electric kiln powered by renewable energy, filled to the brim before every firing.

Fresh from the kiln

This season's latest pieces. Some go quickly.

All the work

Six weeks from a lump of clay to your table

Throwing, slow drying, bisque firing, glazing, a final firing at 1,280 °C. Each stage has its own pace, and the clay often decides the rest.
